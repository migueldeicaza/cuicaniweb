Your privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy explains how the developer of Cuicani ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Cuicani app, its online catalog, this website, or our support form.

Cuicani does not require an account. Your imported music and practice settings are stored on your device. The app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to help us understand usage and fix problems. Optional microphone input is processed on your device.

Information collection and use

  • Music and settings. Cuicani stores your library, imported scores, and app and practice settings locally. Importing a music file does not upload it to us. Files you keep in iCloud Drive or another file provider are also subject to that provider’s policies.
  • App usage. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect automatic usage events and technical information, such as app interactions, session activity, app version, device and operating system information, and an app instance identifier. We use this information to understand how Cuicani is used and improve it. The app does not use Firebase’s advertising ID support, and advertising personalization signals are disabled.
  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics collects diagnostic information when something goes wrong, including crash traces, app and operating system versions, device characteristics, installation identifiers, and information about the app’s state around a crash. We use these reports to investigate errors and improve reliability.
  • Online catalog. Searching for or downloading music sends the necessary requests to our catalog service, including search terms, filters, or the score requested. Network services receive technical information such as your IP address. Service logs may include request times, routes, status codes, and service credential identifiers. We use this information to deliver music, protect the service, and troubleshoot problems. If you submit a catalog report, we receive the information you include.
  • Support. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to submit, such as your message, contact details, and any attachments. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or files you do not want us to receive.

Microphone and MIDI input

Features that listen to your piano ask for microphone permission. Microphone audio is analyzed on your device to detect notes; Cuicani does not record it to an audio file or send it to us. MIDI input is used on your device for music practice. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device’s Settings.

Website and cookies

This marketing website does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may collect technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our support link opens Google Forms. Google may process information and use cookies when you visit the form or other Google services. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Your browser’s settings let you manage cookies for those services.

Service providers and sharing

We use providers for analytics, diagnostics, hosting, and support. These providers process information needed to provide their services. For details about Firebase’s handling of data, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate privacy protections.

Retention and security

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app; copies in device backups or file providers may remain under your control. We retain support correspondence and service records for as long as reasonably needed to answer requests, maintain and secure the service, and meet legal obligations. Analytics and crash data are retained according to the applicable service settings and provider policies.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Our providers may process information in countries other than yours.

Your choices and requests

You can use the app without creating an account, manage your music files, and control microphone access in device Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal information, or to object to its use. Contact us through the support form to make a privacy request. We may need information to verify your request, and we may be unable to identify an individual’s analytics or crash records from a name or email address alone. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of online services and support forms. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion.

Links to other services

Links to external websites and services are governed by their own privacy policies. Please review those policies when you leave Cuicani or this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Cuicani evolves. We will publish changes here and update the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice.

Contact us

For questions about this policy, privacy requests, or app support, contact the Cuicani developer through our support form.
